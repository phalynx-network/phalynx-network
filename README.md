## What is PhalynX?

A decentralized protocol for evidence-based dispute resolution with economic incentives, jury consensus, and Bitcoin-denominated settlements.

- **Protocol core** — Trustless evidence pipeline with cryptographic attestations, reputation-scaled bonds, and commit-reveal jury voting.
- **Evidence and jury** — Encrypted evidence storage, Merkle-tree anchoring, and decentralized jury panels with economic stake alignment.
- **Settlement on Bitcoin** — Buy-and-burn verdict tokens settled in BTC with transparent payout ordering and reputation impact damping.
